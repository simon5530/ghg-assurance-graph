# SoftwareX contribution brief — qualified alpha

- Typed evidence graphs distinguish calculated results from public reported totals.
- Validation separates failed checks from evidence that cannot be assessed.
- Scope 2 alternatives remain separate during cross-period numeric comparisons.
- Public-company assertions retain source hashes and explicit missing lineage.
- Offline evidence packages support repeatable inspection without an AI service.

## Reuse versus project contribution
RDFLib, PROV-O, pySHACL, Pydantic, Pint and RO-Crate provide established infrastructure.
The project contribution is their bounded organizational-GHG integration, selected
constraints, version comparison and reproducible evidence workflows—not invention
of provenance, carbon accounting, SHACL or causal inference. The original ACME
benchmark remains synthetic. Public-company disclosures are a separate evaluation
of ingestion, missingness and inspection, not an independently assured inventory.

## What the evaluation cannot establish
No source activity/factor reconstruction is possible from aggregate totals alone.
No company-wide compliance, source truth, real abatement, autonomous causal accuracy,
novelty superiority, practitioner adoption or AI performance is established. Reported
external assurance language is a company/source assertion, never this tool’s opinion.
Research Gate A remains HOLD; v1.0, archival DOI, current official template and human
submission decisions are not supplied by an alpha tag.
