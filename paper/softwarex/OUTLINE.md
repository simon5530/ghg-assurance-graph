# SoftwareX working manuscript content

Draft only; not the official template and not submission-ready.

## Abstract
Organizational GHG results require inspectable links to sources, factors, methods
and review decisions. GHG Assurance Graph provides an offline Python evidence
layer using typed records, RDF/PROV, selected SHACL/domain constraints, bounded
version comparison and portable research objects. A public synthetic three-version
benchmark exercises provenance, ten seeded defects and nine change components.
The encoded defects yield 10 true positives and no false positives or negatives;
annotation-assisted attribution reconciles nine expected components exactly. These
results demonstrate the specified fixtures, not blind generalization, independent
assurance or causal interpretation. No full standards conformity is claimed.

## 1. Motivation and significance
See [problem](../../docs/PROBLEM.md) and [related work](../../docs/RELATED_WORK.md).

The integrated organizational workflow remains a research hypothesis; Gate A HOLD

## 2. Software description
[Architecture](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) separates canonical records, graph
construction, allowlisted queries, SHACL/domain checks, Decimal attribution and
evidence export. Scope 2 alternatives cannot be added; unsupported raw-gas and
market-based cases fail rather than receive invented factors.
[CarbonDiff](../../docs/CARBONDIFF.md) uses a declared ordered decomposition and
UNKNOWN residuals, not uniquely causal explanation. [Evidence packages](../../docs/EVIDENCE.md)
include canonical JSON, graph exports, RO-Crate metadata and independently checked
digests. Hashes do not authenticate source claims.

## 3. Illustrative examples and results
The [README workflow](../../README.md) generates, queries, validates, compares and
exports ACME fixtures. [Results ledger](RESULTS_LEDGER.md) records quantitative
observations and limitations. Public development/holdout labels are not a blinded
evaluation. Seven semantic row labels are supplied as assertions; two electricity
components are mechanically decomposed. Without these assertions, subsequent-year
UNKNOWN is +300 kgCO2e signed and 500 absolute, against total delta +210.

### Public aggregate-disclosure evaluation
A separate `reported-disclosure/1` path preserves published totals as assertions
without fabricated calculation lineage. A reproducibly selected Taiwanese company
and a held-out company test explicit fact mapping, per-series comparisons, missing
evidence and portable inspection. This evaluates workflow breadth, not causal
accuracy or independently assured emissions. The raw source reports are not bundled.

## 4. Impact and limitations
No independent users, empirical research adoption or practitioner validation is
claimed. Generic JSON/CSV interchange is not a tested PACT/openLCA/Brightway adapter.
Bounded graph tools are not an LLM experiment. ISO Part 1 source inspection informs
[coverage limits](../../docs/STANDARDS_ALIGNMENT.md), not certification.

## 5. Conclusions and availability
The experimental software provides reproducible selected-source evidence operations.
Research gap, external validity, broader matching, uncertainty, complete reporting
and organizational controls remain unresolved. MIT code and synthetic fixtures are
available in the public repository. No archived-release DOI exists.
