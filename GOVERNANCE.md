# Governance

The repository maintainer (GitHub handle `simon5530`) coordinates scope and reviews.
Contributors receive credit for actual contributions. No affiliation, ORCID, degree,
professional accreditation, or scholarly authorship is inferred from participation.

Phase changes require a recorded evidence-based gate decision. Research findings
may result in GO, HOLD, or PIVOT; absence of evidence is not GO. Changes to domain
semantics need independent domain review when available and explicit uncertainty
otherwise. Breaking decisions use an ADR; conflicts of interest must be declared.

A single-maintainer project has limited review independence. Do not describe
maintainer approval or automated checks as external assurance. AI systems are tools,
not accountable maintainers or paper authors. Human authors approve submissions.
